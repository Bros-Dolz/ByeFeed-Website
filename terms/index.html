8. Pacts, progress and XP

A pact commits one blocking group to its existing settings for the duration you choose. The app shows the duration and XP reward before you start. Our server sets its start and end times and decides whether it qualifies for completion and its reward.

Pausing blocking or changing, disabling or deleting the committed group can break a pact. Cancelling ends it without the reward. A pact belongs to the installation that started it; losing that installation’s local record, for example by clearing Android app data or reinstalling, prevents that installation from claiming completion. Network interruptions can delay reporting or completion.

XP, achievements, days won and streaks are progress indicators. They have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred or redeemed for money. Streaks are calculated on your phone; they are not independently verified by the server. No leaderboard placement or XP award is guaranteed if the requirements are not met or the necessary records are unavailable.
