The problem

Feeds are built never to end.

Short-form video and infinite scroll are designed so there is always one more thing to watch. You open an app to answer one message, and the feed is waiting on the way in. Willpower is a poor match for that, so ByeFeed puts a rule you chose between you and the feed, and holds it for you.

  • Endless by design

    A feed has no bottom, so there is never a natural moment to stop.

  • Tangled up with what you need

    Messages, groups and search live inside the same apps as the feeds.

  • Deleting the app is overkill

    Uninstalling costs you the useful parts too. ByeFeed lets you keep them.

What it does

Block what pulls you in. Keep what you need.

Set your rules once. ByeFeed enforces them the moment something matches, without asking you again.

  • Block apps

    Pick the apps that take your day. Open one during a block and the block screen is already there.

  • Websites & keywords

    Block whole sites or any keyword in Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Opera, DuckDuckGo, Vivaldi and Kiwi, and optionally inside other apps’ built-in browsers. Blocked pages send you to a search engine you choose.

  • Short-form feeds

    Switch off Shorts, Reels, Spotlight and more across nine apps, while messages and the rest of each app keep working.

  • Groups & schedules

    Put apps, sites, keywords and feeds in a named group. Block it outright in Strict mode, or allow a set number of timed sessions, on the days and hours you pick.

  • Friction that sticks

    Countdowns on the block screen, a pause you can limit or switch off, a PIN on your settings, and optional Prevent Uninstall. A weak moment takes more than one tap.

  • Statistics & widgets

    See where today went, and label apps productive, neutral or distraction. Put blocks and screen time on your home screen with widgets.

  • Backup & sync, if you want it

    Sign in with Google or email to back up your setup and sync it across devices. Skip it, and everything stays on your phone.

  • Stays on

    Blocking runs in the background and starts again by itself after your phone restarts or the app updates.

How it works

Three steps, then leave it alone.

  1. Make a group

    Choose the apps, websites, keywords and feeds to block, and give the group a name.

  2. Set the terms

    Strict, or a budget of timed sessions. On a schedule, or all the time. Add a PIN if you know you will argue with yourself.

  3. Get on with your day

    ByeFeed works in the background and steps in only when something on your list opens.

Privacy

Private by design.

ByeFeed only watches for the triggers you set, and it does that on your phone.

  • Only your triggers

    ByeFeed reacts to the apps, sites, keywords and feeds on your block list and ignores everything else. Nothing it checks is stored or uploaded.

  • No ads. No analytics.

    No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and no advertising ID. Pro is how ByeFeed pays for itself.

  • An account is optional

    Every blocking feature works without signing in. An account only adds cloud backup and sync.

  • Delete it yourself

    Delete your account from inside the app and it is gone right away, together with its cloud backup.

Questions

Before you install

Is ByeFeed available on iPhone?

Not at the moment. ByeFeed is Android only (Android 7.0 or later), and there is no App Store version.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Continue without an account during setup and every blocking feature still works. Nothing is created on our servers and your setup stays on your phone. An account only adds cloud backup and sync across devices.

How does ByeFeed know what to block?

It watches only for the triggers you set: a blocked app opening, a blocked site or keyword in your browser, or a short-form feed you switched off. Everything else is ignored, and the check happens on your phone without storing anything. Exactly how it works.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, tap your account under Account, then Delete account. It takes effect immediately and removes your cloud backup. The Delete Account page covers every case, including what to do if you can’t open the app.
